The World Map View

WorldMapView is a full-screen panel, hidden until opened. Add the component to a UI object on your canvas and assign its Panel; it builds the rest of its rig itself.

Opening

  • Open key — M by default. It is a plain field on the component: change it, or set it to None and drive Open(), Close() and Toggle() from your own input code.
  • Opening the map pauses nothing and changes no game state: it toggles its own panel only. If your game wants pause behaviour, that decision stays yours.
  • Closing returns the exact prior pan and zoom. Reopening shows the map as you left it.

Moving around

  • Drag to pan. Scroll to zoom toward the cursor — the point under the cursor stays under it, and the offset clamps so the map never pans past its edges at zoom ≥ 1.
  • Center on Player (the CenterOnPlayer() call, wired to a button in your UI if you want one) returns the view to the followed target.

What it shows

The whole bounds fitted at zoom 1: background, fog overlay, icons. The fog rules apply exactly as they do on the minimap — the world map shows only explored fog, so the shape on it is the shape of where the player has actually been. A Manual marker stays hidden until it is revealed.

Selecting markers

Icons are clickable. A click selects the marker: the view's info line shows the marker's label, and the MarkerSelected event fires with the marker — the hook a quest system uses to react when a player taps a place on the map (Quest Hooks).
