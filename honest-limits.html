Honest Limits — What v1 Is Not

Everything here is a deliberate v1 boundary, stated so you can plan around it instead of discovering it. This page also ships inside the package as Documentation~/WhatV1IsNot.md.

  • No camera-capture or render-texture map mode. The map picture is a background image you place and calibrate; everything live on it is an icon. If your game needs a literal top-down render of the scene, this kit is not that tool — that is the trade that keeps it pipeline-proof and cheap.
  • URP and Built-in only. HDRP is not supported in v1.
  • No built-in save system. Fog state is exposed as data (bytes or a Base64 string) for your own save system to store. MiniAtlas Pro writes no files and owns no save slots.
  • One active map. v1 supports a single map per scene setup. Multi-map games (one map per level area, streamed worlds) are out of scope.
  • No marker clustering, heatmaps, route or path drawing, annotations, or player-placed pins. Markers are points with categories and rules.
  • No networked marker sync. Markers are local. A networked game feeds its local state in through the public API.
  • No procedural map art. The kit calibrates your background image to your world; it does not draw the world for you.
  • No in-world 3D waypoints and no HUD compass. v1 is the two map views and their icon layer.

Claim discipline

  • Fog save/load is a plain feature of the kit. It is never claimed as unique to this product — other minimap tools save fog too.
  • The allocation claim is scoped: per-frame view updates allocate nothing. Setup, reveals, and icon acquisition may allocate.
  • Tested means tested: Unity 6000.5.4f1 and Unity 2022.3.22f1, on Windows, in the editor. No other platform or device verification is claimed.
